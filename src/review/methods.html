Which approach do I use?

Many exam questions are won by choosing the right model or calculation first. Learn the clues, then practice picking.

The intuition

Each question type leaves clues. Words like "expected ratio" point to a Punnett square or chi-square. "Which way does water move" means water potential. "Allele frequencies in a population" means Hardy–Weinberg. "How fast" means a rate. Name the approach first; the arithmetic is the easy part.
