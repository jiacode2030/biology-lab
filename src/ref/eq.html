Statistics

x̄ = Σx / n
sample mean
s = √[ Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1) ]
sample standard deviation
SEx̄ = s / √n
standard error of the mean
95% CI ≈ x̄ ± 2·SE
approximate 95% confidence interval
χ² = Σ (o − e)² / e
chi-square, df = categories − 1

p + q = 1

p² + 2pq + q² = 1
Hardy–Weinberg genotype frequencies
P(A and B) = P(A) · P(B)
independent events
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A and B)
either event
